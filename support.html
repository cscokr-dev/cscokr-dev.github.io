How do I play?

Tap a tile to open it. A number tells you how many mines touch that tile. Press and hold to place a flag, or use the shovel/flag switch below the board. Tap an open number whose flags are complete to open the tiles around it. Open every safe tile to clear the stage and collect the photo.

What are stars, crowns and hidden illustrations?

Each stage has three difficulty tiers. Clearing a tier earns its photo. Clearing a tier without using Continue earns a crown. Three crowns on one stage unlock that model's hidden illustration.

Continue is greyed out

Continue needs a rewarded ad. If no ad is available (for example when offline) it is disabled; Retry is always available.

I bought No Ads but still see ads

Open Settings and tap Restore while online. Purchases are tied to your store account and are separate on Android and iOS.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it; it cannot be recovered by the developer.

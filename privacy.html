Mine Gallery — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Developer: 어번 게임즈 · Contact: cscokr@gmail.com

Mine Gallery ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what data is handled when you use the app.

1. Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows advertising. Advertising partners may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads.
  • The app offers one optional purchase ("No Ads"). Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; the app never sees your payment details.
  • The app is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress (cleared stages, crowns, best times), settings (sound, haptics, language) and a local event log used for gameplay statistics are saved in the app's private storage. This data is not uploaded by the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

4. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch you are asked for consent to personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy in the app.
  • iOS: the app asks permission before tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • No Ads: purchasing "No Ads" removes banner and interstitial ads.

5. AI-generated content

All models shown in the app are fictional characters. Photos and illustrations are generated with AI tools and do not depict real persons. Any resemblance to a real person is unintentional.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer holds no personal data about you. To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data held by advertising partners, use the links in section 3.

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
